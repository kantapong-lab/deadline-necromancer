## 1. Market size and trend

- [estimate] Thai board game market about 74.12M USD in 2024, forecast 198.09M USD by 2033, CAGR 10.33% (IMARC, forecast not measured). https://www.imarcgroup.com/thailand-board-games-market · checked 2026-11-16
- [estimate] Different scope: playing cards + board games 272.04M USD in 2024, board games segment 203.70M USD, CAGR 5.61% to 2032 (DataBridge). https://www.databridgemarketresearch.com/nucleus/thailand-playing-cards-and-board-games-market · checked 2026-11-16
- [estimate] Marketeer table: Thai board game market 2,329M THB (2024), 2,562M (2025), 2,795M (2026); headline says about 3,000M THB. Underlying source of the table not seen. https://marketeeronline.co/archives/479226 · checked 2026-11-16
- CONFLICT: the three estimates differ by up to about 3x (74M USD is about 2,600M THB at roughly 35 THB/USD [inference], so IMARC and Marketeer roughly agree; DataBridge is far higher because of scope). Older Marketeer articles said no clear market value existed because the market is niche. For a community pitch use "billions of baht, growing" at most, and label it an estimate.
- [fact, qualitative] Statista describes Thai board games as moderate growth. https://www.statista.com/outlook/amo/app/games/board-games/thailand/ · checked 2026-11-16 (figures paywalled)
- [fact] Board game shops rose quickly over 4-5 years from 5-6 shops, mostly in Bangkok (older Marketeer piece, undated in snippet, so the year is unknown). https://marketeeronline.co/archives/373799 · checked 2026-11-16
- [fact] During COVID-19 some shops' monthly board game sales doubled, from 1.5-2M THB to 4M THB. Same source family as above (Marketeer), exact article not confirmed.
- [fact] Thai PBS: board game trend in Thailand revived and widened over the past few years; many ages play together. https://www.thaipbs.or.th/news/content/321161 · checked 2026-11-16
- [fact] Marketeer: high-income adults collect designer-looking board games; organisations and brands use board games for learning, relationship building and staff skills. https://marketeeronline.co/archives/373799 · checked 2026-11-16
- [fact] Example venue: Kopi-O board game cafe, Bangkok, 800+ games, 40 THB/hour. https://bk.asia-city.com/node/96811 · checked 2026-11-16
- [not found] Current count of Thai board game cafes/shops; Thai board game designer/publisher count; party-game share of sales.

## 4. Thai gamers and workers (habits)

- [fact] Oct 2025 GMO Research & AI / Z.com survey, 474 Thai internet users aged 16-60: nearly 90% played mobile games. Non-player motivators: rewards 41.7%, easy-to-learn gameplay 31.3%. Small online sample, skews to internet users. https://gmo-research.ai/en/resources/studies/2025-report-moblie-gaming-th · checked 2026-11-16
- [fact] 2020 survey: about 4 in 5 Thais play video games, highest ratio in ASEAN. https://www.khaosodenglish.com/life/2020/11/06/survey-4-5-thais-play-video-games-highest-ratio-in-asean/ · checked 2026-11-16
- [fact] Daily News: board games build teamwork and management skills and simulate scenarios. https://www.dailynews.co.th/news/3993950/ · checked 2026-11-16
- [not found] Thai-specific data on office/IT workers' team-building or social gaming habits. Results were generic English listicles (AhaSlides Thai blog, DeskTime, Slack), not research. Gap; suggest the pitch runs its own short poll in the target community.
- [inference] "Easy to learn" being a top reason non-players start games supports a low-friction join flow (phone, no download), consistent with the product's phone-based design. Not a measured Thai party-game finding.

## 5. User pains with existing social-deduction games (global, English; no Thai voices found)

Sources are reviews/blogs, not Thai users. Short quotes from search summaries:
- Early elimination: "if you're killed off early, the rest of the game is incredibly boring" (reviewer paraphrase in results); eliminated players face "lots of downtime". https://leagueofgamemakers.com/the-problems-with-werewolf · https://mechanicsofmagic.com/2023/04/13/critical-play-werewolf-2/ · checked 2026-11-16
- Dead players cannot speak to the living under classic rules, which makes long games dull for them. Same sources.
- Moderator quality: a weak moderator makes the game unpleasant; a good one is the game's strength. https://www.meeplemountain.com/reviews/blood-on-the-clocktower-review-helping-friends-murder-each-other · checked 2026-11-16
- Player count: classic Werewolf needs about 7+ players plus a moderator, 10-12 ideal. Same sources.
- Existing fixes: One Night Ultimate Werewolf (no elimination, about 10 min, app moderator). https://www.wargamer.com/one-night-ultimate-werewolf/review · checked 2026-11-16
- [not found] Thai-language user quotes (Pantip, Facebook groups, BGG Thai reviews). Search did not reach them; recommend manual search on Pantip and Facebook groups.
- [inference] Pitch-relevant: the product's screen-based Moderator and 6-12 player range address the moderator and player-count pains; rules doc governs elimination behaviour, so check it before claiming "no boredom after elimination".

## Insight for the pitch
Thai board game market is described as growing (estimates only, sizes disagree) and has events (TK Park, Thailand Board Game Show, university fairs) usable as community channels. Hard evidence on Thai social-deduction demand and on office-worker habits is missing, so the pitch should rely on playtest sign-ups and a self-run poll, not market stats.
