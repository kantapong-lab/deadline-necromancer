## 3. Implications (inferred, not sourced)

- [inferred] A Thai-language, web-based room game with no install fits the "free to try, host-led" pattern. Jackbox shows hosts will pay about $25-30 per pack. A 570-950 THB shelf price is the Thai physical anchor.
- [inferred] Among Us's mobile revenue fell sharply after its peak; a free-with-cosmetics model needs a very large audience. For a small Thai community, a free playtest plus optional paid corporate sessions may be more realistic. Corporate team-building price ranges suggest a themed Sprint/Deadline session could be priced per group, but no comparable was found.
- [inferred] Realistic first channels: board-game cafes, the Bangkok Meetup, and the Thai tabletop publishing and awards circuit. Plan to ask Thai players directly for LINE OpenChat and Discord leads, since public search cannot see them.
